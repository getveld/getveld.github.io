Improve the workflow. Then decide whether AI belongs in it.

Veld helps owner-led service businesses improve recurring work that gets stuck, repeated, or lost between people and systems. We find the cause first, then compare practical ways to fix it.

Start with the work

Find the cause before choosing the tool.

When recurring work stalls, gets repeated, or loses information between handoffs, buying a tool first can make the wrong process faster. Veld starts by finding what is causing the delay, mistake, or extra work.

The right answer may be a simpler step, clearer ownership, an existing feature, conventional automation, or a human-reviewed AI test. Sometimes the right answer is to measure first, defer, or leave the workflow alone.

How it works

Start with one workflow. Leave with a clear next decision.

01

20-minute Workflow Triage

A fit conversation about one recurring workflow: what happens, why it matters, who owns it, what evidence exists, and whether a paid assessment is likely to help. It is not a free audit.

02

AI Workflow Opportunity Assessment

A paid assessment of 3–5 priority workflows. Veld maps the current work, separates facts from assumptions, compares practical options, and delivers a ranked 30-day plan.

03

Implementation, if justified

If a change is worth making, Veld can separately scope one observable workflow outcome with acceptance criteria, human review, training, handoff, and rollback.

What you get

A decision plan you can use with or without Veld.

The assessment shows which workflows deserve attention, what is causing the problem, which option fits best, and how to test it without losing control.

What the assessment makes clear

  • What happens now and where the work breaks down.
  • What is known, what is assumed, and what still needs to be measured.
  • Which process, current-tool, automation, or AI option fits best and why.
  • Who owns the next step, what success means, and when to stop.
  • Whether to proceed internally, use another provider, ask Veld to scope the work, or make no change.
