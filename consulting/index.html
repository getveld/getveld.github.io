Workflow-first consulting

Improve the workflow. Then decide whether AI belongs in it.

Veld helps owner-led service businesses find the few process, automation, or AI changes worth testing—with clear evidence, owners, risks, and stop conditions.

Start with the work

Not another AI tool list.

Recurring work can wait, repeat, lose information, or create avoidable rework. The first question is not which product to buy. It is what is causing the friction—and whether changing it is proportionate, safe, and measurable.

A responsible recommendation may be to simplify a step, clarify ownership, use an existing feature, automate conventionally, test AI with human review, measure first, defer, or make no change.

A bounded path

From fit question to an executable decision.

01

Workflow Triage

A 20-minute qualification conversation about one recurring workflow, its consequence, ownership, evidence, data risk, urgency, and decision authority. It is not a free audit or recommendation report.

02

Opportunity Assessment

A paid diagnostic that maps 3–5 priority workflows, separates facts from assumptions, compares intervention paths, and produces a ranked 30-day decision plan.

03

Bounded implementation

If implementation is justified, scope one observable workflow outcome with acceptance criteria, human review, change control, rollback, training, and handoff. Implementation is always separate.

The assessment

A roadmap, not a catalog.

When the evidence supports it, the assessment produces current-state maps, a ranked opportunity matrix, complete recommendation cards, alternatives, risks, assumptions, owners, baselines, targets, guardrails, and stop conditions.

What makes the work useful

  • Observed facts stay separate from estimates and unknowns.
  • Process and current-tool options compete fairly with new automation.
  • High-consequence decisions keep accountable human review.
  • Projected time release is not presented as realized cash.
  • The client can implement internally, use another provider, ask Veld to scope work, or stop.

Proof boundary

Method first. Claims only when earned.

Veld has a documented assessment method designed to identify and prioritize practical workflow opportunities. Veld is building outcome evidence through paid work and does not publish customer logos, testimonials, or realized-outcome figures without measurement, permission, caveats, and approval.

No guaranteed outcome

Veld does not promise savings, revenue, compliance, security, or error-free output. A specific company may already have the workflow handled well, and a responsible assessment can conclude that no change is justified.
