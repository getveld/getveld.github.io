About Veld

Technology experience without technology theater.

Before founding Veld, Steven Davis spent 25 years leading technology teams and delivering systems that had to work outside the demo.

That experience shapes Veld's standard: solve the real problem, be clear about what is proven, and keep people responsible for decisions that affect employees, customers, or money. You work directly with Steven.

01
Start with the need

Understand the person, workflow, and consequence before choosing technology.

02
Prefer the smallest effective change

Simplification and existing tools deserve consideration before new systems.

03
Keep people accountable

Automation should support judgment, not quietly replace it where consequences matter.

04
Measure, learn, and stop when needed

Every meaningful test should have an owner, evidence, guardrails, and a rollback path.
