Susan Personal Gmail Assistant

A private Veld workflow for one authorized Gmail account owner.

Not a public application

This assistant is not offered for public sign-up. It is used only by Steven Davis and Veld's authorized assistant, Susan, to support Steven's own Gmail account at his direction.

What it does

The assistant can prepare private daily-brief summaries and carry out narrowly defined mailbox-maintenance rules that Steven has approved. Those rules may include reading messages, applying or removing labels, archiving or quarantining messages, deleting items after an approved review period, and sending an unsubscribe request when Steven has authorized that workflow.

Authorization

Google OAuth is used so the Gmail account owner can approve or revoke access. Only the intended account owner should authorize this client. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access controls.

Privacy

For details about the Gmail data this assistant may access, how derived information may be stored, and the services involved, read the Personal Gmail Assistant Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this private automation may be sent to susan@getveld.ai.
