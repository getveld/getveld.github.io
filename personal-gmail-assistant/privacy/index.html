Personal Gmail Assistant Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026 · Veld, LLC

Limited private use

This policy applies only to Susan Personal Gmail Assistant, a private automation used by Steven Davis and Veld's authorized assistant, Susan, for Steven's own Gmail account. It is not offered for public sign-up or third-party use.

Information the assistant may access

When the Gmail account owner authorizes the assistant through Google OAuth, the assistant may access Gmail message headers, sender and recipient information, subject lines, message bodies, attachments when needed for an approved task, labels, thread state, timestamps, and other mailbox metadata.

The configured Gmail permissions allow the assistant to read messages, apply or remove labels, archive or quarantine messages, and send email. Sending is limited by Veld's operating rules to an action the account owner has approved, such as a narrowly defined unsubscribe request. Destructive cleanup runs only under the account owner's approved retention and review rules.

How information is used

Gmail information is used only to perform workflows requested or approved by the account owner. These may include preparing a private daily brief, identifying likely spam or unwanted subscriptions, applying quarantine labels, removing quarantined messages from the inbox, carrying out approved cleanup after a review period, and processing approved unsubscribe actions.

Gmail information is not used for advertising, sold, or used by Veld to build marketing profiles or train Veld models.

Storage and retention

The OAuth client configuration and authorization token are stored in an account-scoped directory on Susan's managed computer. The assistant normally queries Gmail when a workflow runs rather than maintaining a separate copy of the mailbox.

Derived summaries, task notes, decisions, and limited message excerpts may be stored in Steven's private Obsidian vault, Hermes Agent session records, and the backups or sync services configured for those private systems. Their retention follows Steven's private note, session, backup, and deletion practices. Messages and labels retained in Gmail remain subject to the account owner's Gmail settings and approved mailbox-maintenance rules.

Services involved

  • Google and Gmail provide authentication and mailbox APIs.
  • OpenAI currently provides the configured AI service used to analyze selected mailbox information and draft summaries or classifications. Provider handling is governed by the applicable account, product, and service terms.
  • Obsidian and any configured private sync or backup service may store derived notes selected for Steven's private vault.
  • Hermes Agent runs the local automation and may retain operational session records needed to execute, audit, and troubleshoot workflows.

The assistant does not intentionally disclose Gmail information to other third parties except as needed to operate these approved workflows, comply with law, protect rights or security, or act at the account owner's direction.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Security

Veld uses administrative and technical measures intended to limit access to the authorized account and private automation environment. OAuth credentials are stored with restricted local file permissions, and automated jobs are configured to use this account-specific credential rather than a shared default credential. No system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Account-owner choices

  • The account owner can revoke OAuth access from the Google Account's third-party access controls.
  • The account owner can ask Susan to pause or remove the automation and delete its locally stored authorization token.
  • The account owner can review and change mailbox-maintenance rules, quarantine periods, and approved unsubscribe behavior.
  • The account owner can request deletion of derived private notes or session records, subject to applicable backup, security, and recordkeeping limits.

Children

This private business and personal-productivity automation is not directed to children under 13 and is not available for public use.

Changes

Veld may update this policy if the assistant's data access, providers, storage, or operating rules materially change. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.
