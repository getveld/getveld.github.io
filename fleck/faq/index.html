Using Fleck

Product FAQ

What is a fleck?

A fleck is a small part of the whole picture: a quick voice note or typed thought captured while the day is happening. One sentence counts. So does a longer vent, memory, or reflection.

What is a daily sketch?

A daily sketch is a readable journal entry shaped from the flecks you captured that day. It is meant to help you revisit what happened in your own words, not replace your judgment or tell you what your life means.

Do I have to write long journal entries?

No. Fleck is built for quick capture. Tap record, say the thought while it is fresh, and move on. Typing is available when you want it, but voice is the main flow.

Can I edit transcripts or sketches?

Yes. Fleck lets you edit transcript text and daily sketch text, so you can clean up transcription mistakes or reshape the entry into something that feels more like you.

Who is Fleck for?

Fleck is for people who want journaling to feel easier and more natural: quick thoughts, busy days, walks, commutes, decompression moments, and the “I should write this down” thought that usually disappears.

How much does Fleck cost?

The App Store shows the current plan prices, renewal periods, and whether an introductory offer is available to you before purchase. Purchases and cancellations are handled through the App Store on iPhone.

Is Fleck available on Android?

Fleck is available for iPhone through the App Store. An Android version is in internal testing and is not publicly available yet.

Is Fleck therapy or mental health care?

No. Fleck is a personal journaling app. It is not therapy, medical care, crisis support, legal advice, financial advice, or professional advice. If you are in danger, in crisis, or need urgent help, contact local emergency services, a crisis hotline, or a qualified professional.

How do I get help or send feedback?

Email support@getveld.ai for help, questions, or feedback about onboarding, privacy, transcription quality, or the voice-first journaling experience.

Privacy

What happens to your audio and transcripts?

Short version: Fleck stores journal entries, transcripts, sketches, and recordings on your device. Transcription, AI sketches, subscriptions, and support may involve third-party services when you use those features.

You control saved recordings. When you record a voice fleck, Fleck saves the recording locally with the transcript. You can delete saved audio while keeping the transcript, and journal exports include text, not original audio files.

Privacy FAQ

Is my journal private?

Fleck stores your entries, transcripts, generated sketches, recordings, and related journal data on your device. Some features use outside processing: audio transcription, AI sketch generation, App Store billing, subscription entitlement checks, and support if you contact us.

Is audio transcribed on my phone or in the cloud?

Fleck uses Deepgram for transcription. With your permission, the audio for that fleck is sent to Deepgram so a transcript can be generated. Depending on your installed Fleck version, the request may go directly to Deepgram or pass through Veld's AI gateway first. If transcription is not used, that audio is not sent for transcription.

Can I delete the audio and keep the transcript?

Yes. For recorded flecks, you can delete the saved audio while keeping the transcript. You can also type flecks instead of recording, and journal exports include transcript and sketch text without original audio files.

Can I delete a fleck completely?

Yes. Fleck lets you delete or discard entries from the app’s local journal view and database. Deleting or discarding a recorded fleck also removes the saved local audio file for that fleck.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. Fleck can export a Markdown file with daily sketch text and fleck transcripts. Original audio recordings are not included in the export. After export, the privacy of the file depends on where you choose to save or share it.

What does Fleck send to AI services?

When you generate a daily sketch, all completed journal text for that day—including transcripts, typed entries, and edits—and up to 30 prior saved sketches may be sent to a Google Gemini service. Depending on your installed Fleck version, the request may go directly to a Google Gemini API or pass through Veld's AI gateway to Google Cloud Vertex AI using a Gemini model. If automatic generation is enabled, Fleck may do this when the app checks for missing or stale sketches. You can turn automatic generation off in Settings and write or generate sketches manually instead.

Is my journal used to train Fleck’s own AI models?

No. Fleck does not use your journal content to train its own AI models. Third-party providers such as Deepgram, Google, RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play process data under their own policies and obligations. See the Privacy Policy for the provider list.

Does Fleck sell my journal content or use ad tracking?

No. Fleck does not sell your journal content and does not use third-party advertising or advertising-identifier-based tracking. Subscription and entitlement services process limited purchase and paywall data to operate paid access.
