Fleck Support

Fleck is a voice-first journal for capturing small moments throughout your day. If you need help with Fleck, have a billing question, or want to report a bug, you can reach us here.

Contact support

Email: support@getveld.ai

We’ll do our best to respond as quickly as we can.

Privacy and terms

Fleck is designed as a local-first journaling app. When transcription or AI sketch features are used, related content may be processed by third-party services needed to provide those features.

Read the Privacy Policy · Read the Terms of Use · Read the FAQ

Subscriptions and billing

Restore a purchase: Open Fleck Settings and choose Restore Purchases. Restore uses the Apple or Google account that originally made the purchase.

Manage or cancel: Choose Manage Subscription in Fleck Settings, or open your Apple App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Deleting Fleck does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds: Apple or Google handles charges and refund eligibility for purchases made through its store. Contact the store shown on your receipt. Veld does not receive your full payment-card details.

Android availability

Fleck for Android is being prepared for Google Play but is not publicly available yet. Veld has not published a verified public Google Play listing URL. Do not install Fleck from unofficial APK sites or download links.

AI services and saved journal data

Turn off future AI processing: In Fleck Settings, turn off Use AI services. This also turns off automatic daily-sketch generation. It stops new AI requests but cannot recall content already processed.

Delete saved audio but keep text: Use the saved-audio deletion action for a recorded fleck. The transcript remains in your journal.

Export: Choose Export Journal in Settings to create a Markdown text export. Original audio files are not included. After export, the file is controlled by the app or destination you choose in the share sheet.

Backups and sync: Fleck has no user-created Fleck account or Fleck cloud-sync service. Journal data is stored in app storage and may be copied or restored by device backups according to your device and platform settings.

Report a problem

Email support@getveld.ai with:

  • your device model and operating-system version;
  • the Fleck version and build shown at the bottom of Settings;
  • what you were trying to do and what happened instead; and
  • whether the problem happens again after reopening Fleck.

Please do not send private journal text or recordings unless support specifically needs an example and you are comfortable sharing it. Synthetic examples are usually enough.
